## Full description (Google Play)

FotoMat 2000 brings the classic mall-photobooth back to your pocket.

**Take a strip.**
Line up your face, snap four photos, and FotoMat auto-assembles them into a classic vertical film strip — just like the booth at the arcade.

**Make it yours.**
- 6 retro filters (B&W, Sepia, Grayscale, Film, Ink & Dreamy)
- A sticker pack of hand-picked reaction stickers — drag, drop, resize & rotate
- Mirror preview so you always look the subject the same way the camera does

**Save & share.**
- Export full-resolution strips to your photo library
- Share straight to your messaging apps

**Secure sign-in.**
Unlock with your device's native biometrics — Face ID or fingerprint — so your session stays private to you.

Works fully offline; optional cloud galleries sync when you're online.

Requires a device with a camera. No ads. No tracking.

## Full description (Apple App Store)

**FotoMat 2000 — the photobooth in your pocket.**

Snap a classic four-shot photo strip, dress it up with retro filters, and drop your favorite reaction stickers on top.

- 📷 **Classic booth mode** — 4 rapid shots become a vertical film strip
- 🎨 **6 retro filters** — Dreamy, B&W, Sepia, Film, Ink & Grayscale
- 🧸 **Sticker pack** — drag, resize and rotate the best reaction stickers
- 🖼️ **Share** — save to Photos or share to any app
- 🔐 **Private** — optional Face ID / fingerprint unlock, nothing tracked

FotoMat 2000 runs entirely on your device — your photos never leave it unless you share them yourself.
