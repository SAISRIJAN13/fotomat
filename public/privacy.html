Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what FotoMat 2000 collects when you use the online photobooth, why it collects that information, and how it is stored. Please read this policy together with the Terms & Conditions.

1. Information we collect

FotoMat 2000 may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your username, email address, and a password hash with its salt.
  • Session information: a browser-stored session token and the associated account identifier.
  • Photo strips: when you complete a four-shot strip, the resulting photos, filter name, and username are saved to the account's Supabase database.
  • Stickers: stickers are combined with the photo strip before it is saved. We do not maintain a separate sticker profile.

2. Camera and photo processing

The camera feed is used only while you are taking photos. Photos are processed in your browser with the selected filter. A single last-shot download is created in your browser; a completed four-shot strip is uploaded to Supabase when you save it.

3. How we use information

We use collected information to create and authenticate accounts, keep you signed in, store your completed photo strips, and provide the photobooth features. We do not use your photo content for advertising.

4. Storage and service providers

Account data and saved photo strips are stored using Supabase. The service also loads the Supabase JavaScript client and Google Fonts from third-party CDNs. These providers may process technical request data in accordance with their own policies.

5. Cookies and local storage

FotoMat 2000 uses browser local storage for its sign-in session. It does not currently use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

6. Security

Passwords are transformed into a salted PBKDF2 hash before storage. We use secure connections and reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your password or session information.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain account and saved-strip information while needed to provide the service. There is currently no self-service deletion control in the photobooth. Contact the FotoMat 2000 operator to ask about accessing or deleting your information.

8. Children and third-party links

You should have permission to use the service and to share any photos or stickers you upload. FotoMat 2000 is not intended for children who are not allowed to create accounts under applicable law. Links to external services are subject to those services' own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of FotoMat 2000 means you accept the updated policy.

10. Questions

For privacy questions or a request concerning your information, contact the FotoMat 2000 operator using the contact information provided with the service. This page is a practical starting point and may need review for the laws that apply to your location.
