How it works

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Allow camera access.
  3. Pick a filter, then press the shutter.
  4. Choose your timer (3s, 5s, or 10s) and wait between shots.
  5. Download your finished strip.

Tips

  • Filters are baked into the photo at capture time.
  • Drag stickers onto your strip to decorate it.
  • Choose a film style for your strip color/theme.
  • Save a single shot or the full 4-up strip.
